About the Club

A building located on the east side of the campus stands out among the surrounding structures: the University Club. It is the only place at NOSHAM University that adopts the style of California's medieval modern residence. The uniqueness of the University Club lies not only in its architecture. It is a peaceful retreat away from the hustle and bustle of the main campus, where one can enjoy a moment of solitude or have a gathering with colleagues. Members can choose from various dining options - including delicious dishes prepared by our in-house chefs - or opt to enjoy beverages either indoors or on the terrace.
